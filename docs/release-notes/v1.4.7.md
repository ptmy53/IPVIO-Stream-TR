## Highlights
- Live TV works again over providers with a wrong certificate (after you agreed)
- Switching back to a playlist you used before is instant
- Smoother scrolling through the Live TV categories
- Diagnostics messages are in English

## Fixed
- A channel from a provider whose certificate does not match its address could stall ("connection stalled"). The app now goes straight to the unencrypted address you agreed to, and addresses with special characters are no longer broken when it does

## Improved
- Playlist switch: a playlist shown before is shown again at once, without an empty screen or loading. If the device needed the memory, the normal switch runs
- Live TV: the channel list follows a category only after you stop on it (a little later than before)
- Settings > Diagnostics: all messages are English now

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- The fixes are not yet tested on many devices. If a channel still stalls, send the log from Settings > Diagnostics.

**Full list of changes:** see `CHANGELOG.md`, section 1.4.7.
