## Highlights
- Live channels that sat in Movies (for example Sky Sci-Fi, Sky Krimi) are back in Live TV
- Tabs on long settings pages: Design & color, Player and Storage
- One filter page for languages, groups, categories and genres, with a short explanation per tab
- A "Reload" button on every playlist

## Fixed
- Live TV channels whose address has no "/live/" part no longer end up in Movies because their category is called "Sky Cinema", "Film" or similar. Reload your playlist once after the update so it is sorted again
- The refresh option "Every few hours" was shown in German in every language; it is translated now
- The button "Open content languages" opened Live TV & Guide instead of the language filter

## Improved
- Settings > Design & color: tabs Color theme, Background, Animations, Display size, Adjust screen and Font instead of one very long page
- Settings > Player: tabs Playback, Intro and next episode, Clock, Network and frame rate, Second picture, Source & quality
- Settings > Storage: tabs Cache, History and Backup
- Settings > Genres & categories: all playlist filters in one place. Content languages decides what is loaded at all, Ignore groups hides whole provider tags, Show and hide categories hides single ones, Automatic genres sorts by genre. The language list is no longer repeated under Playlists
- Settings > Playlists: each playlist has its own "Reload" button; on a playlist that is not the main one it becomes the main list and is downloaded again

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- Reload the playlist once (Settings > Playlists > Reload) so live channels are sorted into Live TV again.
- The new settings tabs and the sorting fix are not yet tested on many devices.
