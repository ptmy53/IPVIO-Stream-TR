## Highlights
- Live sync: favorites and history on all your devices (Google Drive, off by default)
- Live TV: move provider categories with Up/Down
- Live TV: Back and focus fixed on Fire TV
- Faster start, smaller app (about 11 MB instead of 19 MB)

1.4.2 was published without notes, so this list also covers 1.4.2.

## New
- Live sync (Google Drive): switch it on in Settings > Storage > Google Drive on each device. Changes upload a few seconds later and download about every minute while the app is open. A safety copy is kept before each merge
- Live TV: a provider category can float (menu entry "Move with Up/Down"): Up/Down move it, OK or Back puts it down. New menu entry "Move to the end"
- Live TV: channel row size small / normal / large (1.4.2)
- Settings > Filters: search categories (e.g. "EU | DE") and switch all hits on or off at once (1.4.2)
- API keys can be entered from the phone with a QR code (1.4.2)

## Improved
- Faster and smaller app: about 15 % faster cold start, APK about 11 MB instead of 19 MB (1.4.2)
- Settings > Filters: switching categories no longer lags; changes are applied with "Apply" or when you leave the page (1.4.2)
- Home screen: movie and series rows follow the order and hidden categories of the category page (1.4.2)
- SubDL subtitle search finds episode subtitles and season packs, with clearer error messages (1.4.2)
- Search: darker background behind the info text, results line up with the search field
- The update card is smaller and sits at the bottom right, so it no longer covers the content

## Fixed
- Live TV: Back in the channel list goes to the chosen category; Back from a stream returns to the last channel and category; OK and Right enter the channel list again (Fire TV)
- Settings > Filters no longer crashes when switching categories (1.4.2)
- Adult titles no longer appear in "Recently watched" rows (1.4.2)

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- Live sync is new and not yet tested on many devices. It needs a Google account and is off until you switch it on.
- Some new texts (phone key entry, SubDL messages) are not translated yet.
