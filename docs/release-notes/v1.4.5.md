## Highlights
- Faster first setup of big Xtream playlists (live, movies and series load together again)
- Smooth scrolling through the Live TV category list
- The screen stays on while a video is paused
- Faster browsing and a quicker start (no extra online lookups while you browse)

## Improved
- Live TV: the channel list follows a category only after you stop on it, so holding Down in the categories no longer stutters. Right or OK still opens the channels at once
- Xtream playlists: live channels, movies and series download at the same time again. A part that the provider refuses is fetched again on its own, so these providers still load completely
- Browsing no longer asks for age ratings; they are looked up when you open a title
- The stream info on the detail page starts only after 1.5 seconds, so a quick look or an immediate Play opens no second connection to your provider
- Languages are read only when they are shown, which speeds up the start
- Player: a pause keeps the screen on; the sleep timer still lets the TV go to sleep

## Fixed
- Settings: the profile circle on the home screen opens "Profiles" again, and "About", "Reset all" and "Donate" open their own pages

## Changed
- The "Sublists" settings page is removed. Saved sublists stay on the device, but an active sublist filter is switched off once, so playlists show all channels again. Categories can still be hidden under "Genres & categories"

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- The speed improvements are not measured yet and have not been tested on many devices.
- Settings > Diagnostics > Log shows a "Start:" line with start-up timings; send it if the start is still slow.
