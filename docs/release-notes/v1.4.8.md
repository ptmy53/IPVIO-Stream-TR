## Highlights
- New: VPN with WireGuard, also for the video stream (Settings > General)
- New: your own DNS server (Settings > General)
- New: Settings > Sports Hub, and OK on a goal popup jumps to the game
- Every playlist has its own Edit window and its own update plan

## New
- **VPN:** Settings > General > VPN connects the device through WireGuard. Download the WireGuard file (.conf) from your VPN provider, choose it, and connect. The video stream goes through the VPN too. The key is stored encrypted and never shown.
- **Own DNS server:** Settings > General > DNS server. Use Cloudflare, Google or Quad9, or enter your own address (an IP address, or an https address for DNS over HTTPS). Handy when a provider is blocked by your internet provider. If the server does not answer, the app uses the DNS of the device.
- **Sports Hub settings:** a new page Settings > Sports Hub. Choose the sports and competitions you follow (switched-off ones are not queried), the alerts and their delay, which tabs of the Sports Hub are shown and which one opens first.
- **Jump to the game:** when a popup tells you about a kick-off or a goal of your teams, OK opens a channel that shows the game, if the app finds one. Can be switched off.
- **26 more competitions:** English divisions and cups, Scottish, Dutch, Portuguese, Turkish and Belgian leagues, national cups, Nations League, Euro, World Cup, Copa Libertadores, NWSL, WNBA, college basketball, LPGA and DP World Tour. They start switched off; switch on what you follow in Settings > Sports Hub.
- **Edit window for every playlist:** in Settings > Playlists every playlist has only "Reload" and "Edit". "Edit" opens a window with name, content (Live TV and movies, Live TV only, movies and series only), backup servers, "Make main list" and "Delete". Changes count only when you choose "Apply".
- **Own update plan for every playlist:** in the Edit window choose "Update playlist": at every start, every few hours, daily, weekly, by hand only, or follow the general setting. A playlist that is not the main list is updated in the background only if it has its own plan.

## Improved
- The app starts faster: the texts of your language and the stored playlist are prepared while the first screen is built, and the silent update waits a few seconds.
- "Reload" on a playlist that is not the main list loads it in the background and no longer makes it the main list.

## Good to know
- Install over the old version; playlists, favorites, history and settings are kept (same signature).
- New in this version and not yet tested on many devices: the VPN (not checked on Fire TV), the new sports competitions (their ESPN addresses are not verified, some may show no games) and the own DNS server (it does not cover the video stream, but the VPN does).
- There is no kill switch: if the VPN connection drops, the traffic continues without it.
- Nothing was measured for the faster start.
